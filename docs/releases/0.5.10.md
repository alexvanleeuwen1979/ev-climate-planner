# EV Climate Planner 0.5.10

A reminder appears five minutes before the confirmed final climate cycle ends, with +15, +30 and +45 minute extension actions.

- Enabled by default. Disable under Settings > Notifications > Reminder 5 minutes before the end.
- The phone notification includes Wear OS actions. Enable this app's notifications in Galaxy Wearable or your watch companion app.
- Timing follows Kia's start confirmation. Extensions update the period and its countdown and schedule a reminder for the new final cycle.
- Stale or duplicate actions cannot extend another period. Stops and completed periods invalidate the reminder.
- Interface text is available in Dutch, English, German, French and Spanish.

Emulator tests passed for final-cycle eligibility, all three extension actions, countdown updates and duplicate-action rejection. Release build and release lint passed. Watch display and delivery have not yet been verified on a physical watch. No live vehicle command was sent during these tests.

Signed with the existing release certificate. The accessibility-service removal from 0.5.9 is included.
